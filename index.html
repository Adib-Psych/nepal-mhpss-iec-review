01 · One poster sample

Common reactions after a disaster · English V4 · SAMPLE watermark

What we are trying to communicate

Reactions can differ after a frightening experience. People deserve support without being required to recount what happened. Small, feasible actions and appropriate help-seeking are offered—not a diagnosis or a promise of rapid recovery.

The fictional cast shows sadness, worry and sleep difficulty, followed by company from someone the person chooses, gentle movement on safe ground and a manageable shared task.
