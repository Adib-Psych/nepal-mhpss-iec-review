Welcome to the IEC review space

Six chapters of information materials for communities affected by the floods and for the people who support them, in English and Nepali, shared with partners for review before they are finalised.

Help us check the Nepali translation

The Nepali texts are draft machine translations. Native Nepali speakers are welcome to check them row by row against the English; this is voluntary and unpaid. We plan to acknowledge contributors, by name or by organisation as each chooses, in the printed package of these materials and on the project website.

After you agree, you go straight to the proofreading page of Chapter 1, where five short steps show how it works. You can move to any chapter from there.

Please read before you enter

  1. Draft materials for partner review: not final and not approved for use in the field.
  2. You are welcome to share this link with others who can give feedback; everyone is asked to agree to these points before entering. Please do not print or distribute the materials themselves.
  3. The Nepali versions are machine translations awaiting review by native speakers.
  4. The illustrations are fictional, AI-assisted and developed under human direction.
  5. Your name, organisation, email, feedback and acknowledgement choice are stored to coordinate the review and credit contributors.
