05 · Decisions requested from reviewers

  1. Message and format: Is the proposed outcome focused enough? Poster, short series or facilitated material?
  2. Technical content: Common-reaction wording, optional sharing/grounding, medication advice and urgent-help presentation; grief-worker scope, ambiguous loss, follow-up and urgent handover.
  3. Local fit: Names, clothing, perceived age, diversity, uniforms and role boundaries; appropriate languages and community reviewers.
  4. Production and clearance: Applicable WHO Nepal / MoHP / partner process, branding, authorship, rights and internal templates.
  5. AI-assisted illustrations: Are these allowed, and what disclosure, rights documentation and human-illustrator review are required?
  6. Validation: Intended-reader testing, accessibility, physical print proof and verified referral routes before distribution.
