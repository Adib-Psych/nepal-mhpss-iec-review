# After the Flood · MHPSS Nepal IEC review space

Public draft-review website; not WHO-endorsed or approved for public health distribution. Curated samples only. No sensitive information or working archives. Illustrations AI-generated under human direction; technical and cultural/language review pending. Official sources are linked, not mirrored. Search exclusion is advisory, not access control. Anyone can open or download public content.


Since 12 November 2026 the home page is the review space for the After the Flood series: one page per chapter (poster, leaflet, Health Talk Guide, translation and proofreading, feedback form), overview and characters. Only approved products are listed; see `PUBLISHING.md`. Content lives in `data/series.json` and `data/translation.json`. The 9 October consultation set is kept at `archive-2026-10-09.html`.
